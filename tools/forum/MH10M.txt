---- What it is ----
MH-10M 47 turns the C47 into a Casio MH-10M, the small desk calculator with cost, selling price, margin and tax
keys. Its 10-digit display (7-segment digits, the decimal point, the thousands marks, the indicators) and its
keypad are drawn on the screen; each key you press shows the Casio key it stands for, inverted. It calculates the
way the Casio does: algebraic entry, left to right with no precedence (2 + 3 x 4 = gives 20), results cut (not
rounded) to the 10 digits shown, the constant of =, the Casio's %, a memory, cost / sell / margin and tax.

---- How to use it ----
Load MH10M and press XEQ MH10M. Then use the C47's keys as the Casio's:
  0-9 .  + - x /   as they are                 ENTER   =
  +/-              change sign                 <-      the Casio's > (the last digit keyed off)
  F1 F2 F3         COST  SELL  MAR             F4 F5 F6   %  TAX-  TAX+
  the second row (the six keys under the soft keys): MRC  M-  M+  C  AC  RATE SET
  x<>y             OFF: the end; the value shown is put in X
Examples: 1 / 3 = shows 0.333333333 and x 3 = then 0.999999999, as on the Casio. 1500 + 15 % gives 1725.
COST, SELL and MAR (the margin on the selling price): key two of them, the third is worked out: 1000 COST 25 MAR
shows the selling price 1333.333333. Tax: set the rate with RATE SET, the rate, RATE SET; then 1500 TAX+ at 8 %
gives 1620 and TAX+ again shows the tax itself (120); TAX- takes the tax out. MRC recalls the memory, MRC twice
clears it; M+ and M- finish the calculation first. E (an overflow or a division by 0): only C (go on with the
value) and AC work. C clears the number being keyed; AC clears everything but the memory and the tax rate.

---- What it changes on your calculator ----
Your stack and LASTx are given back at the end, with the value shown pushed into X (as after a calculation).
Registers R00-R19 are not touched; R20-R91 are used and cleared at the end. The memory and the tax rate are kept
between runs in two variables, MHMEM and MHTAX (delete them to forget them). The screen is cleared at the end;
GRMOD 0 and GRFNT 20 are set; WSIZE is set to 64.

---- Programs and labels ----
One program, MH10M, with one global label (MH10M). Every routine inside is a local label (LBL 00-10 the digit
glyphs, 11-85 the keys by their KEY? codes, called with XEQ IND, 86-96 the operations), so nothing else is added
to your program catalogue. It uses the STRUCT commands (IF / ELSE / ENDIF, DO / WHILE / ENDDO), ATEXT, GRFNT and
AGRAPH of the C47 / R47 firmware. Some KEY? codes (ENTER, x<>y, +/-, <-, the operators, the second row) are not
yet confirmed on a calculator: see the README.
