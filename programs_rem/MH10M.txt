REM MH10M v0.1.0 - MH-10M 47: the Casio MH-10M desk calculator on the C47 / R47
REM The 10-digit LCD (7-segment digits drawn with AGRAPH, the decimal point and the thousands marks) and
REM the Casio's keypad drawn on the screen: the key pressed is shown inverted (XOR) until the next key.
REM It works as the Casio: algebraic entry (no stack), left to right; 10 digits, the results truncated to
REM what the display shows (as the Casio keeps them: 1 / 3 x 3 = 0.999999999); the constant of = (K: for
REM x the first number, for + - / the second); % (a x b % = ab/100, a / b % = 100a/b, a + b % = a + ab/100,
REM a - b % = a - ab/100); MRC (once: recall, twice: clear), M+ and M- (they finish the calculation first);
REM COST, SELL, MAR (margin on the selling price: two give the third); TAX+ and TAX- with the tax rate
REM (pressed twice: the tax amount); RATE SET; E: overflow (the display shows the result / 10^10) or
REM division by 0, only C or AC go on.
REM C47 keys: the digits, . and + - x / as they are; ENTER =; +/- +/-; <- (backspace) the Casio's >;
REM F1-F6 the Casio's top row COST SELL MAR % TAX- TAX+; the second row of the C47 (under the soft keys)
REM MRC M- M+ C AC RATE-SET; x<>y OFF (the end).
REM The tax rate and the memory are kept between runs in the global variables MHTAX and MHMEM
REM (0 STO+ makes them when they are not there).
REM Screen 400 x 240, row 0 = bottom. ATEXT in GRFNT 21 (the title in GRFNT 10). GRMOD and GRFNT take X.
REM The user's stack and LASTx are kept (LocR 10: STOS R.00, LASTx in R.08); at the end the display's
REM value is put in X (the stack lifted, as a calculation) and R20-R91 are cleared (R-CLR 20.72).
REM Registers: R20 X (the value shown), R21 the first number (ACC), R22 the operation pending (86 + 87 -
REM 88 x 89 /, 0 none), R23 the constant's operation, R24 the constant K, R25 1 while a number is keyed,
REM R26 the digits keyed (an integer N), R27 their number, R28 the decimals keyed (-1 no point yet),
REM R29 1 negative, R30 E (error), R31 the memory, R32 the tax rate, R33 the key, R34 the key before,
REM R35 the indicators of the key (COST ... RATE), R36 the indicators shown, R37 the tax amount, R38 1 in
REM RATE SET, R39 COST R40 SELL R41 MAR, R42 the first of two cost/sell/margin keyed (1-3, 0 none),
REM R43 1 when a number was given since the last operation (keyed or MRC), R44 10^decimals keyed,
REM R45 the display's digits (N), R46 its decimals, R47 q, R48 the cell k (0 the right), R49 the cells
REM with a digit (from the right), R50-R59 the digit
REM shown in each cell (10 blank), R60-R69 the mark shown after each cell (1 point, 2 thousands),
REM R70-R72 the glyph's words (left, middle, right columns), R73 a counter, R74 work, R75-R78 work,
REM R79 10^10, R80 the point's word, R81 the thousands mark's word, R84 the AGRAPH word of LBL :RUN:,
REM R85 the keys used ("/11/12/.../"), R86 the value of R45 and R46, R87-R89 the key shown inverted (x, row, height 1 or 2 key rows;
REM 0 none), R90 23 ones, R91 52 ones (the inside of a key).
REM Indicators (R35, R36): 1 M, 2 minus, 4 E, 8 COST, 16 SELL, 32 MAR, 64 TAX+, 128 TAX-, 256 TAX,
REM 512 SET, 1024 RATE.
REM The digits: cell k (0-9, from the right) at x 331 - 31k, 24 columns (4 left, 16 middle, 4 right),
REM the mark after it at x 356 - 31k (4 columns); AGRAPH words from row 145 (64 rows, GRMOD 1: written
REM as they are), segments 48 rows high from row 151.
REM The keypad: key (c, r) (c 0-5 from the left, r 0-4 from the bottom) at x 3 + 66c, row 1 + 29r,
REM 62 x 25 (the + key 62 x 54, rows 0 and 1).
REM Labels: LBL 00-10 the glyphs (0-9, 10 blank), LBL 11-85 the keys (their KEY? codes, XEQ IND 33),
REM LBL 86-89 + - x /, LBL 90-93 the % of each, LBL 94-96 the cost, the selling price, the margin.
